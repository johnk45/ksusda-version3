Our Vision

To raise a generation of godly young leaders who transform their campuses, churches, and communities through authentic faith and servant leadership.

Our Mission

To disciple university students through intentional Bible study, prayer, mentorship, and outreach, equipping them to live for Christ and make Him known.

First Fruit Ministry Departments

See our Departments Information

Welfare Department

+

The Welfare Department is responsible for the well-being of our members. We provide support during times of need, organize social events, and ensure that everyone feels cared for and included in our ministry family.

Health Department

+

The Health Department focuses on the physical and mental well-being of our members. We offer health education, wellness programs, and support services to promote a healthy lifestyle within our Ministry.

Spiritual Development Department

+

The Spiritual Development Department is dedicated to nurturing the spiritual growth of our members. We offer Bible studies, prayer meetings, and other spiritual activities to help individuals deepen their faith and connection with God.

Discipline Department

+

The Discipline Department is responsible for maintaining order and discipline within our Ministry community. We ensure that all members adhere to the principles and values of our faith, and we provide guidance and support to those who may be struggling with behavioral issues.

Uniform Department

+

The Uniform Department is responsible for managing and distributing ministry uniforms for our members. We ensure that all uniforms are of good quality and fit properly, and we provide guidance on proper uniform etiquette and care.

Music Department

+

The Music Department is responsible for leading trainings and organizing music-related activities within our ministry. We normally conduct weekly music training sessions and polishing on Thursdays 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM and Friday from 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM consecutively at LH1.

Gospel Campaign Department

+

The Gospel Campaign Department is responsible for leading evangelism and outreach initiatives within our ministry. We organize gospel campaigns, revival meetings, and other promotional activities to spread the message of Christ.
