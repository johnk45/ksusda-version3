Community Outreach

At Kisii University SDA Church, we believe that faith without works is dead (James 2:26). Our Community Outreach ministry is dedicated to reaching the Kisii community — including vulnerable populations, campus students in need, and families facing hardship — with the love and compassion of Christ.

Through practical acts of service and genuine care, we seek to demonstrate the character of Jesus and bring hope to those around us. Whether it's providing food for the hungry, clothing for the cold, or companionship for the lonely, we are committed to making a tangible difference in our community.

"For I was hungry and you gave me something to eat, I was thirsty and you gave me something to drink, I was a stranger and you invited me in..."
— Matthew 25:35 (NIV)

Our Vision

A thriving community where no one lacks basic needs and everyone experiences the unconditional love of Christ.

Our Mission

To serve the practical needs of our campus and surrounding community through coordinated relief, empowerment, and compassionate outreach.

Our Outreach Programs

🍞 Food Drives

Regular food collection and distribution to families in need. We organize monthly food drives and partner with local vendors to provide nutritious meals.

👕 Clothing Distribution

Seasonal clothing drives to collect and distribute quality clothing to vulnerable populations. Every garment is given with love and dignity.

🏥 Hospital Visits

Our visitation team regularly visits patients at Kisii Teaching & Referral Hospital and local clinics, offering prayers, encouragement, and small gifts.

🎓 Campus Mentoring

We pair experienced members with students who need guidance — academically, spiritually, or personally, helping them navigate university life.

Outreach Departments

Food & Nutrition Bank

+

Coordinates food collection, manages the storehouse, and ensures equitable distribution to needy students and community members.

Dorcas Society (Clothing)

+

Focuses on collecting, mending, and distributing clothing, bedding, and basic supplies to those in need.

Visitation & Comfort

+

Dedicated to visiting hospitals, orphanages, prisons, and the elderly, bringing physical comfort and spiritual hope.

Student Welfare

+

Specifically targets the needs of university students facing financial, academic, or emotional challenges.
