01 — About

Our Ministry

Spreading the gospel on campus and beyond through music, discipleship, and outreach

Christ Messengers Ministry is the youth evangelism arm of Kisii University Seventh-day Adventist Church. We train students in vocal music, public evangelism, and personal ministry — preparing each member to be a bold witness for Christ.

From weekly song rehearsals to gospel campaigns across Kenya, we believe every believer is called to carry the message of hope to a world that needs it.

Our Vision

Shaping Future Christian Leaders

To raise a generation of godly young leaders who transform their campuses, churches, and communities through authentic faith, servant leadership, and a passion for souls.

Our Mission

Discipleship Through Campus Ministry

To disciple university students through intentional Bible study, prayer, mentorship, and outreach — equipping them to live for Christ and make Him known in word and song.

04 — Departments

Our Departments

Click to learn more about each department

Welfare Department

+

Responsible for member well-being, providing support during times of need, organizing social events, and ensuring everyone feels cared for.

Health Department

+

Focuses on physical and mental well-being, offering health education, wellness programs, and support services.

Spiritual Development

+

Nurtures spiritual growth through Bible studies, prayer meetings, and activities that deepen faith.

Discipline Department

+

Maintains order and discipline, ensuring members adhere to faith principles, providing guidance and support.

Uniform Department

+

Manages and distributes ministry uniforms, ensuring quality and proper fit, with guidance on uniform etiquette.

Music Department

+

Leads music training and activities, with weekly sessions on Thursdays 6:30 PM - 8:30 PM and Fridays 5:00 PM - 6:30 PM at LH1.

Gospel Campaign

+

Leads evangelism and outreach initiatives, organizing gospel campaigns, revival meetings, and promotional activities.
