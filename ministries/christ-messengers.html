Christ Messengers

The Christ Messengers Ministry is the evangelistic arm of Kisii University SDA Church, specifically designed to train and deploy bold ambassadors of the gospel across campus, communities, and beyond. Inspired by the Great Commission (Matthew 28:19-20), this ministry equips members with practical skills for personal evangelism, public preaching, and digital outreach.

Our messengers are known for their courage, compassion, and commitment to sharing the Three Angels' Messages. Whether through door-to-door Bible work, open-air preaching, or online media campaigns, Christ Messengers are on the frontlines of soul-winning.

"How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of those who bring good news, who proclaim peace, who bring good tidings, who proclaim salvation."
— Isaiah 52:7 (NIV)

Our Vision

To see every campus student and surrounding community member reached with the everlasting gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To boldly proclaim the Three Angels' Messages, train effective soul-winners, and utilize every available means to bring people to salvation.

Ministry Focus Areas

📢 Campus Preaching

Bold open-air preaching sessions in strategic campus locations, reaching students with the timely message of hope and salvation in Christ.

🚪 Door-to-Door Ministry

Systematic visitation programs in surrounding communities, offering Bible studies, prayer, and literature to families who are searching for truth.

📱 Digital Evangelism

Utilizing social media platforms, WhatsApp groups, and online content to spread the gospel to a wider audience beyond physical boundaries.

🎓 Evangelism Training

Regular training workshops on effective witnessing methods including SALT evangelism, friendship evangelism, and prophecy seminar facilitation.

Christ Messengers Departments

Public Evangelism

+

Organizes open-air campaigns, campus preaching, and large-scale evangelistic efforts to reach the masses with the gospel.

Personal Ministries

+

Focuses on one-on-one evangelism, door-to-door visitation, and friendship evangelism to build personal relationships for Christ.

Literature Evangelism

+

Distributes tracts, books, and study guides across campus and the community to spread truth through the written word.

Digital & Media Evangelism

+

Leverages social media, video content, and online platforms to share the Three Angels' Messages globally.

Training & Equipping

+

Conducts workshops and seminars to train members in effective soul-winning techniques and apologetics.
