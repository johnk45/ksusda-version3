The Sentinels

The Sentinels Ministry draws its name from the biblical concept of the watchman — those who stand guard on the walls of Zion, watching and warning, praying and protecting (Ezekiel 33:7). At Kisii University SDA Church, The Sentinels are a dedicated group of men and women committed to spiritual watchfulness, intercessory prayer, and prophetic awareness.

In a world filled with distractions and spiritual dangers, The Sentinels stand firm as guardians of faith, helping fellow believers stay alert, anchored, and prepared for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

"I have posted watchmen on your walls, Jerusalem; they will never be silent day or night. You who call on the Lord, give yourselves no rest."
— Isaiah 62:6 (NIV)

Our Vision

A spiritually awake and discerning church body, grounded in prayer and prepared for the second coming of Christ.

Our Mission

To stand as spiritual watchmen through ceaseless intercession, prophetic study, and defending the faith against modern deceptions.

What We Do

Prayer Watch

Organized 24-hour prayer chains and early morning prayer vigils covering the church, campus, families, and the nation. We believe prayer is our greatest weapon.

Prophecy Studies

In-depth study of Daniel and Revelation, end-time prophecy, and current events in the light of Scripture. Understanding the signs of the times is our mandate.

Spiritual Alertness

Teaching members to recognize spiritual deception, false teachings, and cultural compromises that threaten the integrity of our faith and mission.

Personal Accountability

Small accountability groups that encourage spiritual discipline, daily devotion, and holy living. Iron sharpens iron, and we grow stronger together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about The Sentinels Ministry and how to join our prayer watch.

What is The Sentinels Ministry?

+

The Sentinels are a team of prayer warriors and spiritual watchmen committed to intercession, prophecy study, and alertness in the last days. We serve through prayer vigils, prophetic education, and spiritual support for the church community.

How can I join the prayer watch?

+

You can join by attending our morning and night prayer sessions, filling out the join form below, or reaching out to one of our ministry leaders. All students and church members who love prayer are welcome.

Do I need prior prophecy training?

+

No prior training is required. We provide study groups, mentoring, and resources for anyone who wants to learn more about Daniel, Revelation, and end-time prophecy.

When are the meetings and vigils held?

+

Regular meetings include daily early morning devotion, Wednesday prophecy classes, Friday night vigils, and Sabbath study sessions. Special prayer events are announced on the ministry calendar.

Can I participate online or remotely?

+

Yes. We often stream our prayer sessions and studies, and we welcome remote participation through our livestream channels and online prayer groups.

Sentinels Departments

Intercessory Prayer

+

Coordinates prayer chains, fasting programs, and special prayer requests for the church and community.

Prophetic Studies

+

Focuses on biblical prophecy, end-time events, and interpreting current world affairs through the lens of Scripture.

Apologetics

+

Equips members to defend the faith, answer difficult biblical questions, and engage thoughtfully with skepticism and other belief systems.

Accountability Groups

+

Organizes small groups focused on personal spiritual growth, overcoming habits, and maintaining a close walk with God.
