New Zion Ministry

Empowering young people through spiritual growth, fellowship, and leadership development

Vision

Our Vision

To raise a generation of godly young leaders who transform their campuses, churches, and communities through authentic faith and servant leadership. We envision a movement of young people fully devoted to Christ, impacting every sphere of life with the gospel.

Join the Mission →
Mission

Our Mission

To disciple university students through intentional Bible study, prayer, mentorship, and outreach, equipping them to live for Christ and make Him known. We are committed to nurturing faith, building character, and sending out passionate disciples.

New Zion Departments

Welfare Department

+

Providing support during times of need, organizing social events, and ensuring everyone feels cared for.

Health Department

+

Focus on physical and mental well-being through health education, wellness programs, and support services.

Spiritual Development

+

Nurturing spiritual growth through Bible studies, prayer meetings, and discipleship activities.

Discipline Department

+

Maintaining order and discipline, providing guidance and support for behavioral issues.

+

Managing and distributing ministry uniforms, ensuring quality and proper etiquette.
