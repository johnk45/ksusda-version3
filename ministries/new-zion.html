New Zion

The New Zion Ministry at Kisii University SDA Church is a passionate and purpose-driven group dedicated to ushering in spiritual renewal and revival within our campus community. Rooted in the prophetic vision of a "new heaven and a new earth" (Revelation 21:1), this ministry embodies the hope of transformation — both personal and communal.

New Zion seeks to create an atmosphere of deep worship, fervent prayer, and intentional discipleship. Our members are committed to living out the values of the early church: fellowship, breaking of bread, apostolic teaching, and prayer (Acts 2:42).

"But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God's special possession, that you may declare the praises of him who called you out of darkness into his wonderful light."
— 1 Peter 2:9 (NIV)

Our Vision

To cultivate a revived, worshiping community that eagerly anticipates and prepares for the Kingdom of God.

Our Mission

To foster deep spiritual renewal through powerful worship, intentional discipleship, and fervent prayer.

What We Do

Prayer Warriors

Weekly intercessory prayer sessions that cover the campus, the church, and the community. We believe in the power of united prayer to bring revival and transformation.

Praise & Worship

Leading dynamic and spirit-filled worship experiences during Sabbath services and special programs. We train worship leaders and musicians for excellence in ministry.

Spiritual Mentoring

One-on-one and small group mentoring for spiritual growth. New members and seekers are paired with mature believers for guidance in their faith journey.

Revival Campaigns

Regular campus revival meetings, Week of Prayer programs, and spiritual retreats that reignite passion for Christ and deepen our walk with God.

New Zion Departments
