Evangelism

Jesus gave His followers a clear commission: "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations" (Matthew 28:19). At Kisii University SDA Church, we take this commission seriously. Our Evangelism ministry equips members to share the Good News of salvation with confidence, love, and relevance — on campus, in the community, and beyond.

"For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth." — Romans 1:16

"How then shall they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe in Him of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without a preacher?"
— Romans 10:14 (NKJV)

Our Vision

To reach every soul on the Kisii University campus and surrounding community with the everlasting gospel.

Our Mission

To train, mobilize, and support every church member to be an active witness for Jesus Christ.

Ways to Share the Gospel

🗣️ Personal Witnessing

Sharing your faith story with friends, classmates, and colleagues. Your personal testimony is one of the most powerful evangelism tools.

📖 Literature Distribution

Sharing Bibles, tracts, books, and magazines like Steps to Christ and The Great Controversy with students and neighbors.

🎤 Public Evangelism

Organizing evangelistic series, revival meetings, and public lectures that present biblical truth in clear, compelling ways.

📱 Digital Evangelism

Using social media, YouTube, and other digital platforms to share sermons, Bible studies, and inspirational content.
