Bible Studies

We would love to study the Bible with you! Whether you are new to the Bible, returning to faith, or looking to deepen your understanding, we offer free Bible study opportunities for everyone. No prior knowledge is needed — just a willing heart and a desire to learn.

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." — Psalm 119:105

"Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth."
— 2 Timothy 2:15 (KJV)

Our Vision

To see every member deeply rooted in the Scriptures, experiencing transformative power in their daily lives.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, structured, and engaging Bible study opportunities that lead people to Jesus and discipleship.

Study Formats

One-on-One Studies

Meet personally with a trained Bible instructor at a time and place convenient for you. Tailored to your questions, pace, and interests.

Small Group Studies

Join a small group of 4–10 people for interactive Bible study in a warm, supportive environment. Groups meet weekly on campus or in homes.

Online Studies

Can't meet in person? Join our online Bible study sessions via Zoom or WhatsApp. We also offer self-paced digital Bible study guides.

Core Study Topics

The Life of Jesus

Discover the love, teachings, and sacrifice of Jesus Christ through the Gospels.

Bible Prophecy

Understand the prophetic books of Daniel and Revelation and how they relate to our times.

Salvation & Grace

Explore what it means to be saved by grace and how to live a transformed life.

The Sabbath

Study God's gift of rest from Genesis to Revelation and its significance today.
