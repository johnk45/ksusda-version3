Youth & Children Ministry

The youth and children are the heartbeat of the Kisii University SDA Church. We are committed to nurturing the next generation of leaders, thinkers, and servants of God through dynamic programs that inspire faith, build character, and foster lasting friendships.

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

"Let no one despise your youth, but be an example to the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity."
— 1 Timothy 4:12 (NKJV)

Our Vision

To see every child and youth saved, equipped, and boldly leading others to Christ.

Our Mission

To provide engaging, biblically sound programs that disciple young people and empower them for service.

Youth Group Activities

Youth Worship

Dynamic praise and worship sessions led by young people, featuring contemporary gospel music, testimonies, and relevant Bible messages.

Youth Bible Study

Weekly Bible study groups where youth explore Scripture together, ask tough questions, and discover how God's Word applies to their daily lives.

Social & Fellowship

Regular social events including game nights, hikes, talent shows, and fellowship meals that build lasting friendships and community bonds.

Service Projects

Youth-led community service initiatives including campus cleanups, visiting the elderly, tutoring younger students, and outreach programs.

Pathfinders & Adventurers

Pathfinders (Ages 10-15)

An action-packed program that includes camping, hiking, nature study, community service, honor classes, and spiritual development. Meetings: Sundays, 2:00 PM.

Adventurers (Ages 6-9)

A fun, age-appropriate program designed to strengthen the parent-child relationship while teaching children about God, nature, and healthy living. Meetings: Sundays, 2:00 PM.

Youth & Children Departments

Children's Ministries

+

Adventist Youth (AY)

+

Focuses on spiritual, social, and physical development of young adults through Sabbath afternoon programs, socials, and community outreach.

Adventist Student Association (ASA)

+

Specifically caters to the needs of university students on campus, organizing vespers, study groups, and campus evangelism.

Uniformed Clubs

+

Manages the Pathfinder and Adventurer clubs, coordinating honor classes, camping trips, and investiture services.
