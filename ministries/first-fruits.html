Our Vision

To raise a generation of godly young leaders who transform their campuses, churches, and communities through authentic faith and servant leadership. We envision a movement of young people fully devoted to Christ, impacting every sphere of life with the gospel.

Our Mission

First Fruit Ministry Departments

Learn more about our departments

Welfare Department

+

The Welfare Department is dedicated to caring for the needs of members within the ministry by offering support during times of difficulty, encouragement in moments of challenge, and practical assistance where needed. It also organizes fellowship and social activities that strengthen unity, foster belonging, and ensure every member feels valued and supported as part of the ministry family.

Health Department

+

The Health Department promotes holistic well-being within the ministry by encouraging healthy lifestyles that support both physical and mental health. It organizes health education programs, wellness initiatives, and support activities aimed at equipping members with knowledge and practices that enhance overall well-being and quality of life.

Spiritual Development Department

+

The Spiritual Development Department fosters spiritual growth through Bible study, prayer meetings, and discipleship activities that strengthen members' faith and relationship with God.

Discipline Department

+

The Discipline Department promotes order, accountability, and Christ-centered conduct within the ministry. It works to uphold the ministry's values, guidelines, and standards while fostering an environment of respect, unity, spiritual growth, and mutual responsibility among members.

Uniform Department

+

The Uniform Department coordinates the provision and management of ministry uniforms, ensuring members maintain a consistent appearance that reflects unity, order, and excellence in service.

Music Department

+

The Music Department oversees the ministry's music programs, fostering worship, musical excellence, and spiritual growth through choir activities, instrumental music, and regular training sessions. Meetings are held on Thursdays (6:30–8:30 PM) and Fridays (5:00-6:30 PM) at LH1.

Gospel Campaign Department

+

The Gospel Campaign Department is dedicated to sharing the message of salvation and the love of Christ with our community and beyond. Through evangelistic campaigns, public outreach programs, revival meetings, literature distribution, and missionary activities, the department seeks to lead individuals to a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ and support the church's mission of spreading the everlasting gospel.
