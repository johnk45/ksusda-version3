First Fruits

The First Fruits Ministry is grounded in the biblical principle of giving God our very best — the first and finest of what we have. Inspired by Proverbs 3:9 — "Honor the Lord with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops" — this ministry teaches and models faithful stewardship as an act of worship and trust in God's provision.

At Kisii University SDA Church, First Fruits goes beyond financial stewardship. We believe in offering our first time, first talents, and first energy to God's work. This ministry equips students and members to manage their resources wisely while maintaining a spirit of generosity and gratitude.

"Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in my house. Test me in this," says the Lord Almighty, "and see if I will not throw open the floodgates of heaven."
— Malachi 3:10 (NIV)

Our Vision

To raise a generation of godly young leaders who transform their campuses, churches, and communities through authentic faith and servant leadership.

Our Mission

To disciple university students through intentional Bible study, prayer, mentorship, and outreach, equipping them to live for Christ and make Him known.

Ministry Focus Areas

Financial Literacy

Workshops on budgeting, saving, and responsible financial management for campus students.

Stewardship Training

Biblical principles of stewardship — time, talent, and treasure management as worship.

Gratitude & Generosity

Community giving projects and harvest thanksgiving celebrations.

Resource Management

Sustainable plans for resource allocation and good stewardship of God's blessings.

First Fruit Departments

Welfare Department

+

Responsible for the well-being of our members. We provide support during times of need, organize social events, and ensure that everyone feels cared for and included in our ministry family.

Health Department

+

Focuses on the physical and mental well-being of our members through health education, wellness programs, and support services to promote a healthy lifestyle.

Spiritual Development

+

Dedicated to nurturing spiritual growth through Bible studies, prayer meetings, and activities to help individuals deepen their faith and connection with God.

Discipline Department

+

Maintains order and discipline within the ministry. Ensures all members adhere to the principles and values of our faith.

Uniform Department

+

Manages and distributes ministry uniforms, ensuring good quality and proper fit, with guidance on uniform etiquette and care.

Music Department

+

Leads training and music activities. Weekly sessions: Thursdays 6:30–8:30 PM (training) and Fridays 5:00–6:30 PM (polishing) at LH1.

Gospel Campaign Department

+

Leads evangelism and outreach initiatives — gospel campaigns, revival meetings, and promotional activities to spread the message of Christ.
