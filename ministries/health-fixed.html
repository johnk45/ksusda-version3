Health Ministry

Seventh-day Adventists have long been recognized as pioneers in health and wellness. Our Health Ministry at Kisii University SDA Church promotes wholistic health — caring for the body, mind, and spirit as an integrated whole. We believe that our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19-20) and that taking care of our health is an act of worship.

Research has consistently shown that Adventists who follow these health principles live longer, healthier lives. In fact, Loma Linda, California — a community with a large Adventist population — has been identified as one of the world's Blue Zones, where people live measurably longer and healthier lives than anywhere else on Earth.

"Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers."
— 3 John 1:2 (NKJV)

About Our Department

The Medical Missionary Department at Kisii University SDA Church combines spiritual ministry with healthcare excellence, serving the university and broader community with compassion and professionalism.

Mission: To train and equip students and church members in the art of medical missionary work, following the example of Christ who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed.

Vision: We envision a community where every individual has access to quality healthcare coupled with spiritual hope. We believe that the gospel and health ministry go hand in hand.

Our department serves as a bridge between the university's academic excellence and the church's mission to serve humanity in need, creating a holistic approach to wellness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our medical missionary programs and services.

Who can join the Medical Missionary Department? +

We welcome all students and church members who have a passion for medical missionary and community service. No medical background is required—we provide comprehensive training for all skill levels. Students from nursing, medicine, public health, and related fields are particularly encouraged to join.

How often do we conduct medical outreach programs? +

We conduct regular outreach programs throughout the year. During the semester, we have training sessions every week on Wednesdays at 6:30 PM. Quarterly medical camps are organized in partnership with local communities, and special health weeks are conducted during specific seasons.

What types of health services do we provide? +

Our services include: health screening (blood pressure, diabetes, BMI), health education seminars, disease prevention workshops, first aid training, community health camps, home visits for elderly members, and spiritual care. We focus on preventive health using the NEWSTART principles (Nutrition, Exercise, Water, Sunlight, Temperance, Air, Rest, Trust).

Is there a cost to join or participate in programs? +

Joining the Medical Missionary Department is free for all students and members. We also provide free health services and training sessions to the community. All our outreach programs are subsidized to ensure accessibility to underserved populations.

How do we combine spiritual care with medical care? +

We believe in holistic health—body, mind, and spirit. While providing medical services, we also offer prayer support, spiritual counseling, and Bible-based health education. Our missionaries are trained to recognize spiritual needs alongside physical health concerns and provide integrated care.

What training opportunities are available? +

We offer training in: First Aid & CPR, Health Communication, Lifestyle Medicine, Disease Prevention, Community Health Assessment, Medical Ethics, Spiritual Care in Healthcare, and Leadership Development. Quarterly seminars feature guest speakers and practical workshops.
