Ministries

The people in the Kisii University Seventh-day Adventist Church stay active through many ministries running simultaneously, all offered for community participation and spiritual growth. Join our teams for service, fellowship, fun, and fulfillment!

We believe that God has asked all Christians to take seriously the commission to tell the Good News. Jesus is not willing that any should perish. Your actions often open a conversation — be so loving and kind that people wonder, "Why are they that way?"

"The church is God's appointed agency for the salvation of men. It was organized for service, and its mission is to carry the gospel to the world."
— Acts of the Apostles, p. 9

Our Ministries

Click on any ministry below to learn more about how you can get involved.

Community Outreach

Reaching out to the Kisii community through food drives, clothing distribution, hospital visits, and campus mentoring programs.

Health Ministry

Promoting wholistic health through the NEWSTART principles — Nutrition, Exercise, Water, Sunlight, Temperance, Air, Rest, and Trust in God.

Youth & Children

Empowering the next generation through Sabbath School, campus ministry, Pathfinders, Adventurers, and vibrant youth programs.

Bible Studies

Discover the joy of studying God's Word — one-on-one, in small groups, or online. All are welcome, regardless of background.

Evangelism

Sharing the Good News of Jesus Christ through campus outreach, evangelistic series, training, and personal witnessing.

New Zion

A passionate ministry dedicated to spiritual renewal, deep worship, fervent prayer, and intentional discipleship on campus.

Christ Messengers

Training and deploying bold ambassadors of the gospel across campus and communities through personal evangelism and digital outreach.

First Fruits

Honoring God with our first and best through faithful stewardship — teaching financial literacy, generosity, and resource management.

The Sentinels

Watchmen on the wall — committed to spiritual watchfulness, intercessory prayer, prophecy study, and preparing for Christ's return.

HOM — Hands On Mission

Action-oriented ministry turning faith into service — building, cleaning, feeding, and supporting communities across the Kisii region.
