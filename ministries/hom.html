HOM — Hands On Mission

HOM (Hands On Mission) is the action-oriented, service-first ministry of Kisii University SDA Church. We believe that the gospel is not just something we preach — it's something we live. Inspired by James 2:17 — "Faith by itself, if it is not accompanied by action, is dead" — HOM mobilizes church members to roll up their sleeves and make a tangible difference.

From building shelters and cleaning communities to providing meals for the vulnerable and mentoring at-risk children, HOM turns faith into action. We partner with local organizations, community leaders, and fellow churches to maximize our impact in the Kisii region and beyond.

"Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me."
— Matthew 25:40 (NIV)

Our Vision

A community transformed by the love of Christ demonstrated through practical acts of selfless service.

Our Mission

To mobilize, train, and equip church members to meet the physical, social, and spiritual needs of the surrounding communities.

Our Projects

🏗️ Community Building

Organizing mission trips to build or repair homes, schools, and community facilities for underserved families in the Kisii region and neighboring areas.

🧹 Campus Clean-Ups

Regular environmental stewardship campaigns including campus beautification, tree planting, and waste management awareness programs.

🍲 Feeding Programs

Weekly meal preparation and distribution to homeless individuals, orphans, and elderly members of the community who need nutritional support.

📚 Education Support

Tutoring programs for local primary and secondary school students, providing school supplies, and supporting educational scholarships for deserving students.

HOM Departments

Construction & Repair

+

Handles physical labor projects, building shelters, repairing homes for the elderly, and maintaining community facilities.

Environmental Stewardship

+

Organizes clean-up campaigns, tree planting initiatives, and promotes sustainable living practices within the community.

Food & Relief

+

Manages the soup kitchen, coordinates food drives, and provides emergency relief packages to families in crisis.

Education & Mentorship

+

Runs tutoring programs for children, coordinates the back-to-school drives, and pairs mentors with at-risk youth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about HOM and how you can get involved in service.

What is HOM and what does it stand for?

+

HOM stands for Hands On Mission — it's the service-oriented ministry of Kisii University SDA Church. We mobilize church members to roll up their sleeves and make a tangible difference in our community through practical acts of service. From building shelters to feeding the hungry, we turn faith into action based on James 2:17: "Faith by itself, if it is not accompanied by action, is dead."

When do HOM activities take place?

+

Regular Weekly Programs:

  • Thursday: Campus Clean-up (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
  • Friday: Soup Kitchen Prep (2:00 PM – 5:00 PM)
  • Sunday: Community Building Project (8:00 AM – 1:00 PM)
  • Sunday: Mentorship Program (2:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

Special projects and events are scheduled throughout the year. Check our Calendar for upcoming HOM events!

How can I join HOM or get involved?

+

We welcome everyone! You can get involved in several ways:

  • Fill out the "Get Your Hands Dirty" form on this page
  • Show up to our weekly activities (times listed above)
  • Contact our ministry director, Bro. Victor Omondi
  • Attend our orientation session for new volunteers

No special experience is needed — just bring a willing heart and ready hands!

What are the main focus areas of HOM?

+

Our ministry focuses on four key areas:

  • 🏗️ Community Building: Building and repairing homes, schools, and community facilities
  • 🧹 Environmental Stewardship: Clean-ups, tree planting, and waste management
  • 🍲 Food & Relief: Feeding programs, meal preparation, and emergency assistance
  • 📚 Education & Mentorship: Tutoring, school supply drives, and mentoring at-risk youth

What special skills do I need to participate?

+

You don't need any special skills! We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds:

  • Construction experience? Great, but not required!
  • Good with kids? Perfect for our mentorship program
  • Love cooking? Join our soup kitchen team
  • General laborer? We need you too!

We'll train you on what you need to know. Just indicate your interests on the volunteer form!

What areas of Kisii do HOM projects serve?

+

HOM serves multiple communities across the Kisii region and beyond, including:

  • Nyanchwa - Medical camps and relief work
  • Suneka - Community building projects
  • Kisii Town - Environmental and clean-up drives
  • University Campus - Regular clean-ups and campus beautification
  • Local Primary & Secondary Schools - Tutoring and mentorship

We partner with local organizations and community leaders to maximize our impact!

Do I need to attend every event to be part of HOM?

+

Not at all! We understand that everyone has different schedules and commitments. You can:

  • Participate in select activities that fit your schedule
  • Commit to regular Thursday clean-ups or Friday soup kitchen prep
  • Join only for special projects and events
  • Contribute financially or provide supplies if you can't volunteer in person

Every contribution, big or small, makes a difference!

How is HOM organized internally?

+

HOM is organized into four main departments:

  • Construction & Repair - Physical labor and building projects
  • Environmental Stewardship - Clean-ups, sustainability, tree planting
  • Food & Relief - Soup kitchen and emergency assistance
  • Education & Mentorship - Tutoring and youth mentorship

When you join, you can work with the department that matches your interests and strengths!

Can I bring friends or family members to volunteer?

+

Absolutely! We encourage it. In fact, many of our best volunteers joined through word-of-mouth. You can:

  • Invite friends and family to join you at any HOM activity
  • Bring them to understand our mission and vision
  • Help them fill out a volunteer form if they'd like to join regularly

Building community is part of our mission. The more hands, the more we can accomplish!
