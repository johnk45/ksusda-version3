Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from guests and church members.

What are your worship service times?

+

Sabbath Services (Every Saturday):

  • Sabbath School: 8:00 AM
  • Divine Worship: 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM
  • Bible Study: 2:30 PM - 5:00 PM
  • ALO AMO Study: 5:00 PM - 6:45 PM

Midweek: Prayer meetings every Wednesday at 6:30 PM

Various ministry meetings are held throughout the week. Check our Calendar page for specific ministry schedules.

How can I join the church or become a member?

+

We welcome everyone to join our church family! You can:

  • Fill out a membership request form on our website
  • Meet with our pastoral team
  • Attend new member orientation sessions
  • Participate in Bible study and fellowship

If you're interested, please contact us or speak with any church member after service.

How can I get involved in ministries?

+

We have vibrant ministries for everyone! Our ministries include:

  • First Fruits - Youth & young adult ministry
  • New Zion - Community outreach
  • Christ Messengers - Evangelism
  • Health Ministry - Wellness programs
  • Personal Ministry - Member care
  • End Time Heralds - Prophetic teaching

Visit our Ministries page to learn more and get involved!

Do you have programs for students?

+

Absolutely! As a campus church, we offer special student programs:

  • Master Guide training programs
  • Weekly Bible study groups
  • Mentorship and discipleship programs
  • Social events and fellowship activities
  • Academic support and counseling
  • Sign language groups
  • Career development workshops

We're here to support your spiritual and academic journey!

How can I submit a prayer request?

+

We'd love to pray for you! You can submit a prayer request by:

  • Using our online prayer request form
  • Calling the church office
  • Emailing us directly
  • Speaking to any church leader in person

All prayer requests are treated confidentially and shared with our dedicated prayer team.

Is there parking available at the church?

+

Yes! Parking is available for all our events:

  • Designated parking areas within Kisii University for regular attendees
  • Visitor parking near the main auditorium
  • Accessible parking for persons with disabilities
  • Additional parking arrangements for special events

For special events, additional parking arrangements are communicated in advance.

What do Seventh-day Adventists believe?

+

The Seventh-day Adventist Church is rooted in the teachings of the Bible. Our core beliefs include:

  • The authority of the Holy Bible
  • Salvation through faith in Jesus Christ
  • The second coming of Jesus
  • The importance of the Sabbath (Saturday) as a day of rest and worship
  • Healthy living and stewardship
  • The great commission to share the Gospel

For more details, visit our "What SDAs Believe" page.

How can I support the church through giving?

+

Thank you for your interest in supporting our ministry! You can give through:

  • Online Giving - Visit our Online Giving page
  • In-person - During worship services
  • Bank transfer - Contact the church office for details
  • Special offerings - For specific ministry projects

100% of tithes and offerings support our ministry, community outreach, and church operations.
