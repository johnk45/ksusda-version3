Study the Word Together

Whether you are new to faith or deepening your walk with Christ, these outlines help you explore Scripture with clarity. Our Bible Studies ministry also offers personal instructors and small groups on campus.

Foundations of Faith

Seven lessons covering the Sabbath, state of the dead, sanctuary, and the second coming — ideal for new believers.

Prophecy & Daniel

Understand visions in Daniel and Revelation and how they point to Christ and end-time hope.

Christian Living

Prayer, stewardship, relationships, and holiness — practical topics for university life.

Available Study Series

  • Discover: Introductory Bible course — request booklets at church or via contact.
  • It Is Written: Online lessons at itiswritten.com/bible-study.
  • Amazing Facts: Free printed guides on key doctrines.
  • Campus groups: Join weekly meetings through our Bible Studies Ministry.

Request a Personal Bible Instructor

One-on-one studies are free and confidential. A trained member will meet you at a time and place that works for you.
