Wholeness — Body, Mind & Spirit

Seventh-day Adventists believe health is a gift from God. Our church promotes simple, biblical lifestyle principles through medical missionary work on campus.

“Pure air, sunlight, abstemiousness, rest, exercise, proper diet, the use of water, trust in divine power—these are the true remedies.” — Ellen G. White, The Ministry of Healing

NEWSTART Principles

  • N — Nutrition: plant-based, whole foods
  • E — Exercise: regular physical activity
  • W — Water: drink plenty daily
  • S — Sunlight: sensible exposure
  • T — Temperance: avoid harmful substances
  • A — Air: fresh, clean breathing
  • R — Rest: adequate sleep & Sabbath rest
  • T — Trust in God: faith reduces stress

On-Campus Programs

  • Health expos and cooking demonstrations
  • Stop-smoking and lifestyle counseling
  • Natural remedies and hydrotherapy talks
  • Community health screening days
