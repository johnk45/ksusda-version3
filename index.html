Worship with Us at Kisii University Seventh-day Adventist Church

We're delighted to offer worship services both in person and online to stay connected with you! Join us via LIVESTREAM every Sabbath at 10:00 a.m. — your presence, whether near or far, means so much to us.

Our dedicated pastor and staff are here to support you, even remotely. If you need assistance or have any questions, please visit our Contact Us page or call us at +254 794 487 419.

Let's come together to worship and grow in faith — we can't wait to share this special time with you!

  • To Share His Message Joyfully

A WORLDWIDE CHURCH FAMILY

The Seventh-day Adventist Church is a mainstream Protestant church with approximately 22 million members worldwide. The Adventist Church operates 173 hospitals and sanitariums and more than 7,500 schools around the world. The Adventist Development and Relief Agency (ADRA) works within communities in more than 130 countries to provide community development and disaster relief.

Weekly Programs

A day-by-day rhythm of worship, study, and fellowship — from Sunday through Friday.

Sunday

Ministry and Family Bible Study

The church introduced these programs to help members study God's word, share the word and pray together.

Monday

Voice of Prophecy (VOP) Teachings

The church introduced a VOP programme so that people can learn God's prophecy in accordance to the biblical teachings.

Tuesday

Year's Group Bible Study

Every year group meets to study God's word and form bonds. A time for accountability, mentorship, and building strong relationships within the university community.

Wednesday

Midweek Vespers

Equipping students with practical health and medical missionary skills to serve communities. Learn how to combine health ministry with the gospel message of hope.

Thursday

Church and Ministry Choir Practice

Church choir members and ministry members meet to practice songs and prepare for the Sabbath worship service.

Friday

Sabbath Preparations

Final preparations for the Sabbath. A Friday vespers is conducted to welcome the Sabbath with prayer and reflection.
