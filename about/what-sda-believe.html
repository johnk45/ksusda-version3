What Seventh-day Adventists Believe

Seventh-day Adventists accept the Bible as our only creed and hold certain fundamental beliefs to be the teaching of the Holy Scriptures. These beliefs, as set forth here, constitute the church's understanding and expression of the teaching of Scripture.

The 28 Fundamental Beliefs

Our beliefs are organized into six categories reflecting the major themes of Scripture:

God (Beliefs 1–5)

  1. The Holy Scriptures
  2. The Trinity
  3. The Father
  4. The Son
  5. The Holy Spirit

Humanity (Beliefs 6–8)

  1. Creation
  2. The Nature of Humanity
  3. The Great Controversy

Salvation (Beliefs 9–11)

  1. The Life, Death, and Resurrection of Christ
  2. The Experience of Salvation
  3. Growing in Christ

The Church (Beliefs 12–18)

  1. The Church
  2. The Remnant and Its Mission
  3. Unity in the Body of Christ
  4. Baptism
  5. The Lord's Supper
  6. Spiritual Gifts and Ministries
  7. The Gift of Prophecy

Christian Living (Beliefs 19–22)

  1. The Law of God
  2. The Sabbath
  3. Stewardship
  4. Christian Behaviour

Last Things (Beliefs 23–28)

  1. Marriage and the Family
  2. Christ's Ministry in the Heavenly Sanctuary
  3. The Second Coming of Christ
  4. Death and Resurrection
  5. The Millennium and the End of Sin
  6. The New Earth

Core Distinctive Beliefs

🕊️ The Sabbath

We observe the seventh-day Sabbath (Saturday) as the biblical day of rest and worship, as established by God at Creation (Genesis 2:1–3) and affirmed in the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:8–11).

✝️ The Second Coming

We believe in the literal, visible, and imminent return of Jesus Christ — the "blessed hope" that gives meaning and urgency to our mission (Titus 2:13).

💚 Wholistic Health

We believe the body is the temple of the Holy Spirit and advocate for wholistic health — caring for the physical, mental, and spiritual dimensions of life (1 Corinthians 6:19–20).
