Welcome to Kisii University SDA Church

We are a vibrant Seventh-day Adventist faith community located on the Kisii University campus in Kisii, Kenya. Whether you're a student, staff member, or a visitor, we warmly invite you to worship with us, grow in faith, and become part of our family.

Sabbath Gatherings

Every Sabbath (Saturday)

9:30 AM Sabbath School — Interactive Bible study in small groups for all ages
11:00 AM Divine Service — Main worship service with praise, prayer, and preaching
After Service Fellowship Potluck — 1st & 3rd Sabbath of each month (all are welcome!)

Weekly Small Groups

Throughout the week we gather in smaller settings for prayer, study, and fellowship. All are welcome — no prior Bible knowledge needed!

Thu 6:30 PM Book Club — Current read: Steps to Christ by Ellen G. White
Sabbath AM Choir Rehearsal & Youth Programs — Before Sabbath School

SDA & Health

Seventh-day Adventists are known worldwide for their emphasis on holistic health — caring for the whole person: body, mind, and spirit.

🏃 Physical

Regular exercise, plant-based nutrition, adequate rest, and avoidance of harmful substances.

🧠 Mental

A positive mental attitude, stress management, lifelong learning, and healthy relationships.

🙏 Spiritual

Daily devotion, prayer, scripture study, and trust in God's plan for your life.

💚 Whole-Person Approach

True health is the harmonious development of physical, mental, and spiritual powers — reflecting the character of God.

Coming to Visit?

We'd love to welcome you! Here's what to expect when you visit:

  • Services begin at 9:30 AM (Sabbath School) and 11:00 AM (Divine Worship)
  • Dress is modest and comfortable — come as you are
  • We are located on the Kisii University Campus, Kisii, Kenya
  • Visitors receive a warm welcome — our greeters will help you find a seat
  • Stay for fellowship lunch on the 1st & 3rd Sabbath
