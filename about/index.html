01/ Who We Are

More than a place to worship.

“We are a home where students become grounded, generous, and ready to serve.”

KSUSDA brings students, staff, and visitors into a living community shaped by Scripture, prayer, music, fellowship, and practical care. We make room for questions, friendship, leadership, and a faith that meets people in everyday life.

03 / What guides us

Faith with both roots and reach.

Know Jesus fully

We create space for honest questions, Scripture, prayer, and a growing relationship with Christ.

Reflect His character

We practice humility, integrity, kindness, and courage in the way we learn and live together.

Share joyfully

We carry the good news through welcome, music, friendship, outreach, and everyday acts of care.

Lead with purpose

We help students discover gifts that can strengthen the church, the campus, and the wider community.
