Nearby SDA Churches

Whether you're a student, visitor, or resident of Kisii County, you'll find a welcoming Seventh-day Adventist congregation near you. Below is a directory of SDA churches in and around Kisii County.

📍 Kisii Town & Surroundings

Kisii Central SDA Church

Location: Kisii Town Centre, along Hospital Road

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

One of the oldest and largest Adventist congregations in Kisii County, serving the town centre community.

Nyanchwa SDA Church

Location: Nyanchwa, near Kisii Teaching & Referral Hospital

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

A vibrant congregation close to the hospital, well known for its health ministry outreach.

Daraja Mbili SDA Church

Location: Daraja Mbili area, Kisii Town

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

A growing church community serving the southern part of Kisii Town.

Mwembe SDA Church

Location: Mwembe Estate, Kisii

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

A warm community church in the Mwembe residential area.

📍 Greater Kisii County

Keroka SDA Church

Location: Keroka Town, along Kisii–Kilgoris Road

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

Serving the Keroka area and surrounding communities.

Ogembo SDA Church

Location: Ogembo Town

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

A well-established congregation in the heart of Ogembo.

Suneka SDA Church

Location: Suneka Trading Centre

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

An active church community with strong youth and children's programmes.

Marani SDA Church

Location: Marani Town

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

Serving the Marani area with vibrant worship and community outreach.

📍 Neighbouring Counties

Nyamira SDA Church

Location: Nyamira Town, Nyamira County

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

The main Adventist church in neighbouring Nyamira County.

Kilgoris SDA Church

Location: Kilgoris Town, Narok County

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

Serving the Kilgoris community and nearby areas.

Migori SDA Church

Location: Migori Town, Migori County

Services: Sabbath School 9:30 AM | Divine Service 11:00 AM

A large congregation in Migori County with an active community presence.
