Worship With Us

Whether you're joining us in person on the Kisii University campus or tuning in from anywhere in the world via our livestream, we welcome you to experience the love, hope, and community of the Kisii University SDA Church.

Service Times

Every Sabbath (Saturday)

9:30 AM Sabbath School — Bible study for all ages
11:00 AM Divine Service — Main worship service
2:30 PM AY Programme — Afternoon youth-led service

Midweek

Tue 6:00 PM Prayer Meeting
Wed 6:00 PM Bible Study
Fri 6:00 PM Vespers / Sabbath Welcome

Watch Online (Livestream)

Can't make it in person? Join us live every Sabbath at 11:00 AM on our YouTube channel. Our services are also archived for later viewing.

How to Get Here

  • By Matatu: Take any matatu heading to Kisii University from Kisii Town. Alight at the main university gate and follow signs to the SDA Church.
  • By Boda Boda: Ask for the SDA Church within Kisii University campus — most riders know the location.
  • By Private Car: Enter through the Kisii University main gate. The church is located near the centre of campus. Parking is available.

What to Expect

First time visiting? Here's what you can expect:

  • Warm welcome — Our greeters will meet you at the door and help you find a seat
  • Modest dress — Come as you are; modest and comfortable attire is appreciated
  • Engaging worship — A blend of traditional hymns and contemporary praise
  • Bible-based preaching — Practical, relevant messages rooted in Scripture
  • Children's programme — Activities for kids during the sermon
  • Fellowship lunch — Stay for potluck on the 1st & 3rd Sabbath

We can't wait to meet you! If you have questions before your visit, feel free to contact us.
