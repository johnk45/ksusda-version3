Special Events

Throughout the year, the Kisii University SDA Church hosts a variety of special events that bring our community together for spiritual renewal, outreach, and celebration. Mark your calendar and be sure to join us!

Week of Prayer

Annual Spiritual Revival

The Week of Prayer is a cherished Adventist tradition — a full week of evening meetings dedicated to prayer, fasting, and spiritual renewal. Visiting speakers bring powerful messages, and the church comes together in united intercession.

When Typically March or October (dates announced each year)
Time 6:00 PM — 8:00 PM each evening, with a special Sabbath programme
Open To Everyone — members, students, visitors, and the community

Evangelism Campaigns

Sharing the Gospel

Our evangelism campaigns are multi-week outreach events featuring nightly preaching, Bible prophecy seminars, health talks, and community engagement. These campaigns often conclude with baptisms and new members joining the church family.

When Twice yearly — typically April and August
Duration 2 to 3 weeks of nightly meetings
Features Prophecy seminars, health screenings, music, and children's programmes

Youth Day / Youth Sabbath

Youth-Led Worship

On designated Youth Sabbaths, the young people of the church take the lead in every aspect of the worship service — from preaching and music to ushering and Sabbath School teaching. It's an inspiring day that showcases the faith and talent of the next generation.

When Quarterly — usually the 3rd Sabbath of the designated month
Highlights Youth sermon, special music, drama presentations, and youth fellowship lunch

Camp Meeting

Regional Gathering

Camp Meeting is a large-scale gathering of Adventist believers from across the region. While organised at the conference level, our church members participate actively and often host preparatory events on campus.

When Annually — dates set by the Kenya Lake Conference
Activities Preaching, workshops, baptisms, ordinations, and fellowship

Community Outreach Day

Serving Our Neighbours

On Community Outreach Days, church members fan out into the surrounding communities to provide free health screenings, distribute food and clothing, visit the sick, and share words of hope and encouragement.

When Monthly — usually the 4th Sabbath afternoon
Activities Health checks, food drives, hospital visits, literature distribution

Baptism Sabbath

Celebrating New Life in Christ

Baptism Sabbaths are joyous occasions when new believers publicly declare their faith through baptism by immersion. The entire church gathers to celebrate and welcome new members into the family of God.

When As scheduled — typically after evangelism campaigns and Bible study series
