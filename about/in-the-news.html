In the News

Stay up to date with the latest stories, features, and media coverage of the Kisii University SDA Church. From community outreach to campus events, here's what's been making news.

Community Health Fair Serves Over 500 Residents

February 15, 2026

The Kisii University SDA Church Health Ministry partnered with local health workers to provide free health screenings, dental check-ups, and wellness education to over 500 community members during the annual Community Health Fair held on campus.

Youth Evangelism Campaign Baptizes 30 New Members

January 20, 2026

A two-week youth-led evangelism series titled "Hope Rising" concluded with 30 baptisms, marking a season of spiritual renewal on the Kisii University campus and surrounding areas.

Read More →

Church Launches Food Assistance Programme

December 5, 2025

In response to rising food insecurity among students, the Kisii University SDA Church launched a weekly food distribution programme, providing essential supplies to students in need every Wednesday afternoon.

Read More →

Choir Wins Regional Music Festival

November 10, 2025

The Kisii University SDA Church Choir earned first place at the Western Kenya Adventist Music Festival, performing a blend of traditional hymns and contemporary worship songs that moved the audience and judges alike.

Read More →

Week of Prayer Draws Record Attendance

October 18, 2025

The annual Week of Prayer, themed "Anchored in Christ," saw record attendance with over 800 people gathering each evening for prayer, worship, and a powerful sermon series by visiting speakers from the East-Central Africa Division.
