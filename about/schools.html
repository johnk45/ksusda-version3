Schools & Education

Education is a cornerstone of Seventh-day Adventist identity. The SDA Church operates one of the largest Protestant education systems in the world, and Kisii University stands as a proud part of that legacy. Our church is deeply connected to the university's mission of holistic education — developing the mind, body, and spirit.

Kisii University — An Adventist Heritage

Kisii University traces its roots to Adventist education in the Gusii region. Originally established as a training institute by Seventh-day Adventist missionaries, the institution has grown into a fully chartered public university while retaining deep ties to the Adventist community.

About Kisii University

Founded Established as Kisii Training Institute, later Kisii College, and chartered as Kisii University
Location Kisii Town, Kisii County, Kenya
Programmes Undergraduate and postgraduate degrees across Education, Health Sciences, Business, Agriculture, Technology, and more
Website www.kisiiuniversity.ac.ke

Adventist Education Philosophy

Seventh-day Adventist education is guided by the writings of Ellen G. White, who taught that "true education means more than the pursual of a certain course of study. It is the harmonious development of the physical, the mental, and the spiritual powers."

Key pillars of Adventist education include:

  • Spiritual Foundation — Christ-centred learning that integrates faith and academics
  • Academic Excellence — Rigorous instruction grounded in critical thinking and inquiry
  • Service to Others — Preparing students to serve their communities and the world
  • Wholistic Health — Promoting physical well-being through lifestyle education
  • Character Development — Building integrity, responsibility, and compassion

Adventist Education Worldwide

The Seventh-day Adventist Church operates over 9,000 schools, 120+ universities, and numerous colleges and secondary schools across every continent. In Kenya alone, the Adventist education system includes primary schools, secondary schools, and tertiary institutions serving thousands of students.

Notable Adventist Universities in East Africa

  • University of Eastern Africa, Baraton — Nandi County, Kenya
  • Bugema University — Kampala, Uganda
  • Adventist University of Africa — Nairobi, Kenya (postgraduate focus)
  • University of Arusha — Arusha, Tanzania

Campus Ministry

Kisii University SDA Church plays a vital role as the spiritual home for Adventist students and staff on campus. We provide:

  • Weekly Sabbath worship and Sabbath School
  • Midweek prayer meetings and Bible study groups
  • Mentorship and spiritual counselling
  • Student-led ministries and outreach programmes
  • Academic and career guidance from a faith perspective
