Find Your Ministry

Media Team

The Media Ministry uses technology and creativity to spread the gospel beyond the church walls. From live streaming and photography to sound management, graphic design, and multimedia presentations, the team ensures that every worship service and church event is communicated clearly and professionally. By serving in this ministry, you help reach both in-person and online audiences with the message of Christ.

Learn More
Chorister Team

The Chorister Team leads the congregation in meaningful praise and worship through hymns, scripture songs, and spiritual music. By guiding worship with reverence, enthusiasm, and unity, the team helps prepare hearts to receive God's Word and creates an atmosphere of joyful worship.

Learn More
Possiblity Ministry

The Possibility Ministry is dedicated to serving and supporting individuals with physical disabilities and other special needs, ensuring they feel welcomed, valued, and fully included in every aspect of church life. Through compassionate care, accessibility, fellowship, and spiritual encouragement, the ministry reflects Christ's love by creating a church where everyone belongs and can grow in faith.

Learn More
End Time Heralds

Join the End Time Heralds and minister through inspiring Seventh-day Adventist hymns and gospel music. Whether you're a first-year student or continuing student, you're welcome to worship, fellowship, and share the hope of Christ's soon return through song.

Learn More
Welfare Programs

The Welfare Ministry reflects Christ's love by caring for the needs of church members and the community through practical support, encouragement, and acts of kindness. Whether assisting those in need, visiting the sick, or organizing welfare initiatives, we strive to be the hands and feet of Jesus. Join us in making a difference through compassionate service.

"Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." — Galatians 6:2 (NIV)

Learn More
Prayer Ministry

The Prayer Band is a ministry devoted to seeking God's presence through faithful prayer and intercession. We pray for the church, students, families, and the community, believing that God hears and answers every sincere prayer. Join us as we grow in faith and uphold one another before the throne of grace.

"Devote yourselves to prayer, being watchful and thankful." — Colossians 4:2 (NIV)

Learn More
