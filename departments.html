Development Department

Strategic growth & resource mobilization

Led by Bro. Kennedy Walucho

The Development Department spearheads church growth initiatives, infrastructure projects, and stewardship of resources. It collaborates with all ministries to ensure sustainable expansion, manages fundraising campaigns, and oversees capital projects that enhance worship experience and community outreach. Under the leadership of Bro. Kennedy Walucho, the department has launched several strategic programs including the "Adopt-a-Project" initiative and annual development symposium.
